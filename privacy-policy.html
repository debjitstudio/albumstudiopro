Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026

AlbumStudio PRO respects your privacy. This policy explains what information may be collected when you visit our website, purchase the software, request support, or submit a customer review.

1. Information We May Collect

  • Contact details you choose to provide, such as your name, phone number, WhatsApp number, or email address.
  • Order information, selected plan, payment reference, transaction status, and billing details supplied by the payment provider.
  • Software licence, activation, device, and support information needed to issue and maintain your licence.
  • Customer review details, including the name or studio name you submit, rating, and review text.
  • Basic technical information generated when you visit the website, such as browser, device, and server logs.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to respond to enquiries, process and verify orders, provide download access and licence keys, deliver customer support, protect the software from misuse, improve the website, moderate reviews, and meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Payments

When an online payment gateway is enabled, payment credentials such as card or UPI details are handled by the selected payment service provider. AlbumStudio PRO should receive only the transaction information needed to verify and fulfil an order. Please review the payment provider's privacy policy before paying.

4. Customer Reviews

When you submit a review, the name or studio name, rating, and review text may be displayed publicly after moderation. Do not include phone numbers, addresses, payment details, licence keys, or other private information in a public review.

5. Service Providers and Disclosure

Information may be shared with hosting, payment, delivery, and technical service providers only as reasonably necessary to operate the service, or where disclosure is required by law. We do not publish private contact or payment details as part of a review.

6. Data Security and Retention

Reasonable technical and organisational measures should be used to protect stored information. Information is retained only for as long as reasonably needed for order fulfilment, support, security, accounting, and legal requirements.

7. Third-Party Content

The website may load third-party services such as YouTube videos, Google Fonts, and content delivery networks. Those services may receive technical information from your browser under their own policies.

8. Your Choices

You may contact us to request correction or deletion of information, subject to records we are required or reasonably entitled to retain. You may also choose not to submit a public review.

9. Contact
