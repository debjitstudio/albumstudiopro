About AlbumStudio PRO

Photography Design Software

AlbumStudio PRO is a digital software product created for photographers, photo studios, and album designers. It brings common photography design tasks together in one desktop workflow.

What We Offer

  • Wedding and event album design tools.
  • Photo editing and enhancement tools.
  • QR-based photo selection workflow.
  • 3D FlipBook preview.
  • Photo frame design tools.
  • Passport photo layout tools.

Our Goal

Our goal is to help photography professionals organise their workflow and prepare designs for their customers more efficiently.
